# Certificate lifecycle and history

The lifecycle report examines the current validated CA inventory's actual DER-derived NotBefore and NotAfter at an explicit UTC time. Warning horizon is 0–365 UTC calendar days, CLI default 90; no implicit SDK clock. Status is NOT_YET_VALID, WITHIN_VALIDITY, EXPIRING_SOON, EXPIRED or INVALID_VALIDITY_INTERVAL. The validity interval includes both endpoints, so the exact NotAfter instant is EXPIRING_SOON (even with zero warning days); immediately after it is EXPIRED. A missing or reversed interval is invalid. Results ordered by expiry then fingerprint; all five count buckets present, max 8192 records after inventory validation. No automatic deletion or policy mutation. This is current inventory examined at a supplied time, not proof that those certificates were present then. Public directory DISUSE is not cryptographic revocation. Dates and fingerprints published by a CA are research claims until checked against DER.

Document.History reuses the existing policy Evaluate logic, returning exact policy ID/version, current anchor evaluation and the visible decisions and provenance reviews for one declared anchor. Decisions recorded after knownAt and reviews recorded after knownAt are excluded, even if retrospectively effective. Future-effective known decisions are SCHEDULED. Older effective decisions are SUPERSEDED; latest applicable or expired decision is explicitly identified. An expired suspension never resurrects an older approval; renewed review/authorization requires a new event. Review status is independently latest-as-known or superseded. Current authorization may still be NOT_ESTABLISHED when a temporally applicable positive decision's evidence is superseded. Raw local evidence and reason fields are included; do not publish this report. History cannot prove that an omitted or rewritten prior document did not exist; cross-revision append-only enforcement is a separate gate. Neither report establishes certificate trust, revocation, source authenticity or production release authorization.

```sh
trust-registry lifecycle --registry ./local-registry --at 2026-11-27T00:00:00Z --warning-days 90
trust-registry policy history --registry ./local-registry --file local-policy.json --fingerprint SHA256 --at 2026-11-26T00:00:00Z --known-at 2026-11-27T00:00:00Z
```

Loaded.Lifecycle and Loaded.PolicyHistory operate on the detached snapshot view, reject evaluation/knowledge after load time, and expose the same local report semantics. Reopen a current validated view per session. No automatic network, monitoring scheduler, revocation fetch, emergency publication, policy change or public DER projection. Tests are synthetic, automatic CI is disabled and Go execution remains pending.
